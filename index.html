My
Process

01

Discovery & Research

We start by diving deep into your goals. I conduct stakeholder interviews and user research to understand the "why" before we touch the interface.

  • User Interviews
  • Competitor Analysis
  • Problem Definition
02

Strategy & Wireframing

Translating insights into structure. I build low-fidelity wireframes in FigJam to map out user flows and information architecture without getting distracted by styles.

  • User Flows
  • Low-fi Wireframes
  • Information Architecture
03

Visual Design

This is where the magic happens. I create high-fidelity UI in Figma, establishing a design system with components, variables, and auto-layout accessibility.

  • High-fi UI
  • Design System
  • Prototyping
04

Handoff & Launch

I don't just send files; I work with your developers. I provide detailed specs, annotated screens, and asset exports to ensure the code matches the pixel-perfect design.

  • Dev Handoff
  • QA Testing
  • Launch Support
